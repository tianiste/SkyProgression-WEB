## Starting out

Before any meaningful foraging, unlock HotF. Complete the Lumber Jack's quest to unlock the Park. On the
Park, talk to the NPC on each island and complete their quest, usually "collect 64 logs of this type". You can buy the
logs from the Bazaar. Then go to Galatea and talk to Hina, which unlocks HotF, and to David Hunterborough, which unlocks
your David's Cloak.

The Heart of the Forest is the foraging skill tree, with buffs to Sweep and fortune you can't forage without. You level
it two ways: Tree Gifts, the rewards you get after breaking a certain share of a tree, and Agatha contest rewards. See
the [Fig guide](/guides/fig/) to forage Fig efficiently.

## Agatha contests

Agatha's contests are the foraging version of Jacob's farming contests. You get points for every 100 logs you break,
including logs from fortune, and for every Tree Gift.

| Rank | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 25 |
| Uncommon | 250 |
| Rare | 1,000 |
| Epic | 2,000 |
| Legendary | 3,000 |
| Mythic | 5,000 |
| Divine | 8,000 |
| Special | 12,000 |

The higher the rank, the better the rewards. Fig logs give far fewer points, but you get around 2x the Tree Gifts and
logs foraging Fig, so it's the choice for high ranks.

## Tokens and whispers

**Tokens of the Forest** unlock perks. Starting at the HotF 1 row, you can only unlock perks connected to ones you own,
within the rows your HotF level allows: row 5 and below at HotF 5.

**Forest Whispers** pay for perks below level 4. You get them from breaking logs, Tree Gifts and Agatha contest rewards;
Fig is the best source.

**Desert Whispers** pay for perks at level 4 and above. You get them the same way, but only while foraging in Torrhus
Canyon.

## Center of the Forest

Center of the Forest unlocks at HotF 5, in the middle of the tree.

| Tier | Cost | Perks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Free at HotF 5 | +1 Token of the Forest, +25 SkyBlock XP |
| 2 | 50,000 Forest Whispers | +1 Axe Ability Level, +35 SkyBlock XP |
| 3 | 100,000 Forest Whispers | +20 Forest Whispers per Fig and Mangrove Tree Gift, +2 per log cut; +20 Desert Whispers per Helix Tree Gift, +2 per log cut; +50 SkyBlock XP |
| 4 | 200,000 Desert Whispers | +5 Tracking on Tree Gifts, +65 SkyBlock XP |
| 5 | 300,000 Desert Whispers | +1 Token of the Forest, +75 SkyBlock XP |

## How to spend whispers

Before Torrhus Canyon, level Center of the Forest to 3. Once you reach Torrhus Canyon, max it; you should be at HotF 5.
The trees below assume this and the token count that comes with it. Other than that, prioritize Sweep and Timber in all
cases. Fortune only matters for making money, and you likely won't make significant coin until you have a maxed
foraging setup.
