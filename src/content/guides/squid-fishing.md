## How to fish

Go to The Park and take the pond to your right. If the rain isn't on, start it through Vanessa.

Cast your rod, use `/pets` to swap to Ammonite, then reel in. Throw the rod back to the water to kill what you catch. For a Carrot King, Water Hydra or Sea Emperor, swap to your [Magic Find setup](/guides/fishing-magic-find/) first. A Skytils key-bind makes pet swapping easier.

Set these Auto-Pet Rules:

- On rod cast: Flying Fish
- On combat entry: Squid, unless your Magic Find pet is active

## Updates since the guide

Checked against the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/May_26).

- **Inklings (May 2026):** fish from a Fishing Hotspot to catch them. A Squid Hat doubles their weight, and they drop the Inkling Shard for Fisherman.
- **Agarimoo and Carrot King (May 2026):** these are now a flat 15% and 5% chance to replace a catch when their conditions are met.
- **Luck of the Sea VI (May 2026):** no longer drops from Water Hydra. Junker Joel sells it for 16 Rusty Coins.
- **Magic Find (Oct 2026):** being tested on the Alpha Network, Hypixel says Magma Lord won't stay best for water fishing.
