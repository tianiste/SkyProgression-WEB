## Tree types

You'll meet big Fig trees, the main ones you farm, and medium and small trees.

For medium trees, jump and break as many logs as you can in each jump. Most can be chopped in two jumps. Some small
trees go in one jump; those beat small trees that take two, but are generally worse than medium trees. Leave medium and
small trees out of your routes until you're no longer infinite on the main 11 big trees.

## Cutting the trunk

You don't need to start at the very bottom. You can aim one or two blocks higher, jump, and still clear the trunk with
your Sweep. The difference is small, and some routes have spots where it's better to teleport near the top and break it
while falling.

When jumping up the trunk, keep breaking logs above eye level rather than stopping once you can't see the lower logs.
That clears it a little faster and makes sure the trunk is gone before you reach the branches, so it can't block you.

While moving up, drift toward your AOTV waypoint on the branches. Don't break the branch you're aiming for, or you'll
fall off the tree.

## Cutting the branches

This is where most of the skill is, and it depends on where you land and where you go next, so these are the core
principles.

- **One hit per branch.** With 400 Sweep you only need to hit one block on each branch, so hit every branch as quickly
  as possible.
- **Keep your movement clean.** Avoid circling the tree, flicking and wiggling your cursor. Take the smoothest, most
  direct path between branches; every extra camera movement costs time.
- **Don't spleef yourself.** Leave the branch you're standing on until near the end, or you'll drop off the tree.

## Think about your next tree

Finish the branches already facing your next tree as closely as you can. You don't want a 180-degree flick before every
teleport. The best ending position depends on your route, so experiment or copy someone who has optimized it.

## Improving your rates

Start slowly and focus on clean movement, consistent branch paths, efficient camera control and positioning for the next
tree. Speed up as these become automatic. Going fast too early just flails the cursor everywhere.

High-level foraging is much more about knowing your route, being consistent and cutting unnecessary movement than about
flashy flicks. Stay smooth and the rates will come.
