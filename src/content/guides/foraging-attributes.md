## Attributes and shards

Attributes are passive buffs you gain by syphoning attribute shards, which you get by hunting mobs in many different
ways. For foraging they give large buffs to Sweep, fortune and Tree Gifts. Without them you will struggle to make a lot
of money. The [attribute reference](/reference/attributes/) lists every one the upgrade path uses, and the
[upgrade path](/guides/foraging-progression/) lists them in order.

A shard goes into your inventory or your hunting box. Right-click one in your hand to send it to the box. `/hb` opens
the box, where you syphon shards to level them up: the golden apple in the bottom right syphons everything. Shift-right-
click a shard to pull all of that shard out if you want to sell or trade them.

## Attribute Menu

`/am` shows your attributes, their levels and how to get each shard. Once you own 25 unique attributes you unlock
advanced mode, with the comparator in the bottom right, which shows every attribute rather than only yours. The easiest
way to reach 25 is to buy shards off the Bazaar.

## Getting shards

Some shards are hunted with a black hole, others caught with a fishing net. Click an attribute in the menu and hover over
the lead to see how. You can also fuse two other shards into one, which some shards require and which saves time or money
on others. Put the shards in your hunting box, go to the fusing machine next to David Hunterborough and click two shards.

## SkyHanni overlay

SkyHanni's Attribute Menu Overlay shows the cheapest attributes to max and how many you need once advanced mode is on.
It is the best way to track what to max next.

## SkyShards

[SkyShards](https://skyshards.com/) works out the most efficient way to get a shard through fusing. Tell it whether you
are an Ironman, which shard and how many you need, and your stats, then follow the fusion tree. It is also good for
fusing for money or hunting XP.

**Its prices can be wrong** because of how it gets them. Check the price of what you are buying before you send the
instabuy.
