## Other Magic Find

- Lucky Clover on a pet: +7
- Ender Dragon pet: +10% to all stats
- Superior armor: +5% to all stats
- Delirium Necklace: +1
- Mayor Jerry: +10% to all stats
- Mayor Aatrox: +20% Magic Find on Slayer bosses

Pet stats are for level 100, without a Lucky Clover.
