## Why fish in a party

A party makes more per hour because everyone in it shares the drops. The most that can Loot Share one mob is five, so a party should have six: the one who kills it and five who share. Shard fishing is the exception, with two players.

## Rules for rare mobs

Never nuke a rare mob. Make sure everyone has dealt at least 1% before you kill it. Nobody needs to deal 90% except the player chosen to kill.

If you fished up the rare mob the party wants to share, you don't need to deal any damage, as it counts as your Sea Creature.

## Looting and Magic Find

The player landing the last hit should have Looting V, as their Looting applies to the drops. Their Magic Find doesn't: each player's own Magic Find counts for their own drops. It only affects Loot Share drops below 5%.

There is a bug that shows the killer's Magic Find on shared items. It is only visual. The guide's author isn't sure it is still in the game.

## Frozen Blaze

Sometimes a whole party wears full Frozen Blaze armor, as its passive deals damage to all mobs in a radius. This is the easiest way to keep up passive Loot Share, usually at Spooky Festivals, Marina Festivals and for Bestiary in the Crystal Hollows.

## Mods

- [SkyHanni](https://github.com/hannibal002/SkyHanni) keeps time and shows how close you are to the Sea Creature cap.
- NWJNAddons (`/ct import nwjnaddons`) counts Sea Creatures in an area and announces the coordinates of a Jawbus or Thunder.
- FeeshNotifier (`/ct import FeeshNotifier`) is useful in all fishing.
