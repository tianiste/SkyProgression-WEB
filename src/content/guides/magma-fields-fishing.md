## How to fish

Go to the coordinates and build the platform from the guide's example video. Fish until you reach your Sea Creature cap, or until 5 minutes are up, and kill them all with your weapon of choice. A mage weapon with good area damage, such as Fire Veil, Fire Fury or a Hyperion, kills them fastest.

Armor, rod and equipment come from [Fishing Progression](/guides/fishing-progression/). Only the pet, bait, rod parts, weapon and attributes change here.

There is no need for bait above 300 Fishing Speed and 100% Sea Creature Chance, unless it is for Double Hook Chance with Marina.

## Drops

On death a Lava Blaze has a 1% chance to drop a Blazen Sphere, and both Sea Creatures have a 0.5% chance to drop a Magma Core.

You need a good [Magic Find setup](/guides/fishing-magic-find/) to profit from this. Blazing Fortune works here too, as both are Magmatic.

## Updates since the guide

Checked against the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Sea_Creatures). Corrected in the text above.

- **Cap (May 2026):** you can have 10 Sea Creatures out at once, on every island. The guide's cap here was 20. The server cap of 60 stays.
- **XP (Oct 2026):** Lava Blaze and Lava Pigman give a fixed 500 Fishing XP. Flaming Worms in the same area give more, see [Worm Fishing](/guides/worm-fishing/).
