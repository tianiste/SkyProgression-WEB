## How to use this list

Attributes work the same as for every other skill: [Foraging Attributes](/guides/foraging-attributes/) explains shards, the Attribute Menu and fusing. **Required** adds a fishing stat everywhere, **Recommended** helps in the place or method named, and **Useful** is worth having once the rest are done.

The [attribute reference](/reference/attributes/#fam-fishing) lists every fishing attribute, including the treasure and Lotus Atoll ones this guide doesn't use.

## Updates since the guide

Checked against the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Attributes) and NEU's shard data. Corrected in the table above.

- **Renamed:** Spirit Axe is now Woodland Ruler, Blazing is Infernal Ruler and Bayou Biter is Bayou Hook. The Sea Wisdom shard is the Loch Emperor Shard, and Fisherman now comes from the Inkling Shard, as Night Squid was removed.
- **Island types:** Crimson Hook now works on all Combat Islands, Bayou Hook on all Fishing Islands, and Blazing Fortune on all Magmatic mobs, not only Lava Sea Creatures.
- **New:** Lake Fishing and Cave Fishing add the Fishing Speed that Forest Fishing gives on Foraging Islands, and Loch Hook adds Double Hook Chance on them.
- **Shard counts:** Thorn (Dominance) is Epic, so 32 shards, and Flare is Epic, so 32 shards too.
