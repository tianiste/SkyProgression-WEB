## Setup

Find a lava pool in the Precursor Remnants above Y 64 and build a box around the lava. Pools are usually at least 4 blocks wide. The more lava, the better.

Bait doesn't matter here: use whatever maxes your stats. Double Hook Chance is the extra stat to chase, with Marina's Double Trouble perk, Burnt Texts on your pet and Titan Line.

The guide's FAQ explains manual pet swapping for Precursor Drone.

## Solo

This is the best way to get XP from worms. Fish until you hit the cap, equip your Squid pet and kill everything.

## Party

For coins you want a full party of six. Once you hit the cap:

1. Everyone except the killer uses the Dreadlord Sword's ability on the worms once.
2. The killer waits 3 to 4 seconds so everyone can hit them, then kills them all.

The killer should have the highest Looting, ideally Looting V. Looting V is only 3.75% more profit than Looting IV.

Place a Totem of Corruption for extra coins and XP from Obfuscated Trophy Fish, which you can filet with Odger on the Crimson Isle. See [Fishing Parties](/guides/fishing-parties/) for how Loot Share works.

## Drops

A worm drops 20 to 30 Rough Sapphire Gemstones, a Worm Membrane at 25% and an Eternal Flame Ring at 0.5%. Loot Share gives 4 to 6 Rough Sapphire, Worm Membrane at 5% and an Eternal Flame Ring at 0.1%. You can turn Worm Membrane into Gemstone Chambers with 100 Membrane and 1 Gemstone Mixture.

Magic Find only helps with the Carmine Dye, at 1 in 2.5 million, and only the killer can roll it. Unless you are the killer going for it, ignore Magic Find. The Rainbug Shard helps the dye chance.

## Updates since the guide

Checked against the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Flaming_Worm).

- **Cap:** you can have 10 Sea Creatures out at once. In a full party of six that makes the server cap of 60, so the party method still works. Solo, your cap is 10.
- **Minister Update (Oct 2026):** Flaming Worms went from 240 to 1,150 Fishing XP, which is why this now works for XP as well as coins.
