## How Trophy Fishing works

Each catch rolls for a Trophy Fish, rarest first. Fish whose conditions you don't meet can't be caught, so fish in a spot where as many as possible can come up, as you get a chance at each. The Blazing Volcano can give you 11 at once.

Because the rarest roll first, a rarer fish you don't want can get in the way of your target.

Modifiers such as a Recombobulator, Aquamarine gems, stars and attributes are all worth it. Whenever there is a Trophy Chance or Fishing Speed Hotspot, use it, unless it stops you catching a fish you need.

If you only want the SkyBlock XP or Magical Power, you can Trophy Fish from Fishing 10 without any Sea Creatures, preferably at Fishing 24 to 27.

## Avoiding Sea Creatures

Sea Creatures interfere until you have Trophy Hunter armor. To avoid them, go somewhere empty and catch 5 Magma Slugs to fill the cap, then damage them so they split into Baby Slugs. These don't despawn unless they die or you change lobbies. You can fish on the Crimson Isle from the Gold Mine island so players are less likely to grief you.

## First 15 fish

1. Start at the Blazing Volcano, fishing in a Geyser while standing 8 blocks above the lava.
2. After Steaming-Hot Flounder and Volcanic Stonefish, move towards the Dojo, equip a Slug pet and a clean Starter Lava Rod.
3. After Karate Fish and Moldfin, which you can both catch in the north-east of the pool at the Dojo, move to the Stronghold. Fish 4 blocks from Sulphur and use Corrupted Bait.
4. Once you have Slugfish, go back to your Flying Fish. Once you have Vanille, go back to your best rod. Once you have Obfuscated 1, use it as bait or go back to Fish Bait.

That gets you the 15 Bronze fish for Bronze Hunter. After that, keep going for whichever spot has the most unique fish.

## The fish

The number is each fish's base chance. Higher means more likely.

**Common**

- Sulphur Skitter, 30: anywhere, within 4 blocks of Sulphur
- Blobfish, 25: anywhere
- Obfuscated 1, 25: with Corrupted Bait, or from Corrupted Sea Creatures
- Steaming-Hot Flounder, 20: Blazing Volcano, with your bobber within 2 blocks of a Geyser
- Gusher, 20: Blazing Volcano, 10 to 15 minutes after an eruption

**Uncommon**

- Obfuscated 2, 20: with Obfuscated 1 as bait
- Slugfish, 15: bobber in the lava for at least 20 seconds, halved by a level 100 Slug pet. Lower your Fishing Speed so it stays in long enough.
- Flyfish, 8: Blazing Volcano, with your bobber at least 8 blocks below you

**Rare**

- Obfuscated 3, 10: with Obfuscated 2 as bait
- Vanille, 8: with a clean Starter Lava Rod. Hold your best rod, then quickly swap to the Starter Rod and cast to keep the Fishing Speed of the other rod.
- Lavahorse, 4: anywhere
- Volcanic Stonefish, 4: Blazing Volcano
- Mana Ray, varies: anywhere, with at least 1,200 Mana. The chance is Mana / 1000.

**Epic**

- Skeleton Fish, 2: Burning Desert and Dragontail
- Soul Fish, 2: Stronghold
- Moldfin, 2: Mystic Marsh and Scarleton
- Karate Fish, 2: Dojo

**Legendary**

- Golden Fish, varies: anywhere, after 8 minutes of fishing, or 3 with the Gold Bait attribute. The chance then rises to 100% over 4 minutes, and catching one resets the timer. It is the hardest of all, so get it passively from Bestiary or normal lava fishing. Don't skip one when it spawns.
