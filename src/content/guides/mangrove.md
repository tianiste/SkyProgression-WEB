## Style

Mangrove has a set cutting order like Fig, or your Sweep is cut in half. Start at the top, breaking the branches
connected to the leaves, and work your way down to the roots in the water. Turn on sweep details in the `/treegifts`
menu and it warns you when you break the wrong part.

Almost never throw your axe. It can help if you missed a branch and a quick throw is faster, but if you can avoid
throwing altogether you will go way quicker. Mangrove's toughness makes throws weak anyway: you won't break many logs
quickly.

## Rates

Rates depend heavily on your skill and ping; some players have overcome ping, but it still strongly affects them. The
best advice is to keep upgrading your gear and go as fast as you physically can.

## Updates since the guide

The guide's author says Fig is better in every metric, and points to the [Fig guide](/guides/fig/). The
