## What should I forage for money?

It mostly depends on ping. Helix is the most profitable, with under 120 ping recommended. Mangrove is second and Fig
third, but on high ping (250+) most players prefer Fig, the least ping-dependent wood. Preference counts too: if you like
foraging Fig, forage Fig.

## Why am I not making the rates top foragers get?

Do you have the best setup for that log, maxed foraging stats, a maxed Heart of the Forest and an efficient route? If yes
to all of them, it comes down to practice and trees per minute. Foraging takes a lot of practice, especially mastering
the best route. You can always send your POV to the Discord for advice.

## Can I still pet level?

Yes, but it has been heavily nerfed. The most advanced foragers XP-share the most profitable pets. Helix's high toughness
ended leveling a pet you have equipped.

## What is the fastest way to max David's Cloak and the Safari Belt?

**David's Cloak:** for Sweep, buy one of every attribute until it's maxed; SkyHanni shows your cheapest shards. Hunting
Invisibugs is the fastest way to upgrade its fortune.

**Safari Belt:** queue the Safari with a team of 4, so you can hunt all four sections of the Critter's Safari efficiently.
Bestiary is shared, so there's no point focusing on foraging fortune: you'll max the milestones for all four biomes at
around the same time.

## What should I spend my coins on?

Follow the [upgrade path](/guides/foraging-progression/) and check your name against it.

## How much Sweep to start big Fig?

400+ is a good start. If you can't fully break the branches, aim further down the branch. See
[Big Fig Trees](/guides/big-fig-trees/).

## Etherwarp or double jump?

Double jump loses 9 Sweep and 65 Fortune (67.5 with a Jade Dragon) without any real benefit. It is harder to control,
less consistent, costs mana, is hit harder by server lag and ping, and is slower. The one real case for it is an Ironman
who doesn't have Etherwarp yet. If you enjoy it, more power to you, but it is just worse than Etherwarp.

## I am running out of mana

Use the Sighted or Demonic power stone. Demonic is best further on in progression, when you want the Strength-to-fortune
perk on the HotF tree.

## Useful tools

- [foragingstats.com](https://foragingstats.com) takes your name and profile and finds the upgrades you missed, updated
  for Torrhus Canyon. It is vibe coded and has minor errors, but it helps find that last bit of fortune or Sweep.
- [waypointermod.com/converter](https://waypointermod.com/converter/) converts route waypoints that don't work with your
  mods.
