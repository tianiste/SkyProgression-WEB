## How to fish

Go to the coordinates and fish in the lava beside the NPC. Fish up your cap of Stridersurfers, then kill them with your axe.

For this to be worth it for Fishing XP, each one has to die in a single hit.

Armor, rod and equipment come from [Fishing Progression](/guides/fishing-progression/). Only the pet, bait, rod parts, weapon and attributes change here, and the Pets section there explains the Dolphin, Ammonite and Flying Fish swap.

There is no need for bait above 300 Fishing Speed and 100% Sea Creature Chance, unless it is for Double Hook Chance with Marina.

## One-hit damage

You need 30,000 damage. That takes 350+ Magical Power, Combat 20+ and the Figstone Splitter. Switching armor for more damage makes it easier. Stridersurfers are Woodland mobs, so Woodsplitter counts.

The guide mentions a bug that lets you kill Woodland mobs with a ranged weapon such as the Soul Whip by using it and swapping to your axe.

## Updates since the guide

Checked against the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Stridersurfer). Corrected in the text above.

- **Cap (May 2026):** you can have 10 Sea Creatures out at once on every island, down from the guide's 30 Stridersurfers. Others fishing with you each have their own 10. The wiki marks Galatea's server cap as 30, unconfirmed.
- **Level and XP (Oct 2026):** level 62, 20,000 health and 775 Fishing XP, down from 800. Flaming Worms now give more XP each, see [Worm Fishing](/guides/worm-fishing/).
- **Requirement:** the wiki lists Fishing 17. The guide's Fishing 19 is when it recommends starting.
