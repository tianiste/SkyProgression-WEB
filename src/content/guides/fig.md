## Style

Galatea's trees have a specific way to be cut or your Sweep is cut in half. Fig is bottom to top: break the trunk and
work your way up to the branches. If you can't tell a branch from a trunk, turn on sweep details in the `/treegifts`
menu, and chat tells you when your Sweep is being halved.

## Method

The best way to cut Fig is to break the big trees; see [Big Fig trees](/guides/big-fig-trees/) and the routes in the
community's public guides. Use God Potion jump boost to break the whole trunk in one jump, then Etherwarp onto a branch
and break the rest from there.

Big trees are better because you mine logs more often than going from small tree to small tree, and if you go quickly
enough big and small trees take a similar time to break.

The trees that come after Fig are in the [Mangrove](/guides/mangrove/) and [Helix](/guides/helix/) guides. For what to
buy, follow the [upgrade path](/guides/foraging-progression/).
